# Default preset certification ledger

The current default preset is not fully certified. This snapshot independently approves 1,867 of the 34,085 frozen assignments: 230 applied corrections, 637 unchanged clue-scroll placements, 939 unchanged equipment placements, 40 ordinary foods and 21 raw-fish Cooking inputs. The remaining 32,218 IDs are unresolved. Agent candidate certificates and proposed revisions do not add to these approval counts.

`approved-assignments.tsv` has one row for every exact numeric ID. `ownerShard` retains the original disjoint review ownership, even after a correction changes category. `decision=revise` denotes an approved correction already verified in the current compiled export; `certify` confirms the existing primary placement. `unresolved` retains the original category/subcategory/tab only as reference. `sourceRevisions` lists the pinned revisions cited by approved rows; `-` means none is approved. The TSV does not reproduce evidence excerpts or supplemental-role claims.

`approved-assignments-manifest.json` records the full decision JSONL hash, TSV hash, source-input hashes, approval-policy hashes and replay-script hashes. Full decisions and the pinned Wiki corpus are local ignored research inputs under `tmp/category-certification/`; a fresh checkout requires the matching frozen inputs to reproduce the audit. The six committed correction policies, unchanged-equipment and food policies and clue rule retain exact-ID evidence and the approved rule boundary. LF attributes preserve the byte hashes across Windows checkouts.

Scope is primary category, subcategory and Ironman tab. Existing tags are preserved unless an approved correction explicitly adds sourced tags. Unknown secondary roles remain unassessed rather than being treated as absent. Cache flags, unbankability, name families, source availability and a successful artifact-integrity gate cannot certify primary semantics.

The clue producer recomputes the strict exact-ID rule from raw pinned pages. The emitter rejects optional clue evidence or semantic fields that differ from the recomputed proof, and it never reads the current export to invent proposed targets. The three gates separately check ownership/schema, evidence integrity and applied targets. Expected results are 34,085 accounted rows, 1,867 actionable decisions, 5,021 exact Wiki evidence records verified, and 32,218 unresolved rows skipped by source/applied checks. They do not independently establish semantic approval.

Production validation and the applied correction details are recorded in `../category-certification-checkpoint-october-3.md`. These files are developer research artifacts and are not loaded by the plugin.

The equipment policy approves the named item's combat function together with its exact active equipment state and a compatible current slot or generic Gear grouping. The main-thread review read the pinned subject definitions and competing uses, replayed the independent raw item/bonus proof, and checked a separate adversarial review. Two draft cases, Castle wars arrow 30694 and Castle wars bolts 30696, were removed because their own definitions restrict them to Castle Wars; Dawnbringer 22516 is also held for encounter-only routing. These exclusions from this tranche are unresolved assignments, not approved runtime exclusions.

`verify-gear-primary-policy.py` checks the approved policy's pinned 2,221-row source-proof artifact, exact row hashes, selected ID/options/equipability, slot and combat bonuses, literal source excerpts and unchanged primary targets. The proof was independently regenerated byte-for-byte before approval. Its successful technical gate does not supply semantic approval; that approval is recorded explicitly by the main-thread review in `gear-primary-approved-policy.json`. The proof and auxiliary review artifacts remain local ignored inputs whose hashes are preserved in the policy.

`root-policy-approvals.json` separately records the approved bytes, complete-content hashes, exact case sets and intended decisions of the nine policy files. The emitter rejects a changed policy instead of silently treating its currently valid Wiki facts as approval. The Gear helper also checks its exact 939-ID cohort against the pinned filter/adversarial record, enforces the three held IDs, and requires the full named-subject lead. A policy change requires independent review and an intentional update to the separate approval record.

A separate exact-state adversarial review found no conflicting current-use restriction among these 40 IDs.

The raw-food policy separately confirms 21 exact raw fish as documented Cooking inputs. Each has an independently read subject definition, direct cooking clause and exact variant binding; secondary bait, quest, offering or output-state roles are unassessed. The output/TSV/manifest paths passed explicitly in the replay command are authoritative. Older local default-name artifacts are not interchangeable checkpoints; use the manifest whose output path and hashes match the selected decision file.
